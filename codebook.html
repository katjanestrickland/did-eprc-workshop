Codebook

Both downloaded datasets contain these six variables

Variable Definition
district_id
  • District identifier from 1 to 832
  • The same identifier follows a district across all seven years
year
  • Study year from 1 to 7
  • Not a calendar year
treatment_start_year
  • First treated year is 5 or 6
  • The traditional DID dataset has only Year 5 adopters
  • 0 means never treated
outcome_std Simulated achievement measured in SD units
treated_ever 1 if treatment_start_year is greater than 0, otherwise 0. Constant within each district
treated 1 if treatment_start_year is greater than 0 and year is at least treatment_start_year, otherwise 0

Both treatment indicators are included in the downloads so participants can begin the analysis directly

EPRC Methods Workshop Series

Difference-in-Differences in Education Research

A Practical Workshop on Traditional and Staggered DID, with Hands-on R Applications

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · 12:00-2:00 PM Eastern
In person: Norman Hall, Room 2707 · Register for Zoom

Learn how to use difference-in-differences (DID) to evaluate education policies and programs. This hybrid workshop covers traditional and staggered DID designs, comparison groups, parallel trends, treatment-effect interpretation, and diagnostic checks. Education examples connect these concepts to an R lab using two-way fixed effects (TWFE) and staggered DID estimators.

Workshop Schedule

Time Session Focus
12:00-1:00 PM Lecture Traditional and staggered DID methods.
1:00-1:30 PM Hands-on R lab TWFE and staggered DID estimators with guided practice.
1:30-2:00 PM Questions & discussion Interpretation and applications in education.

Open to graduate students, faculty, researchers, and staff. No prior DID programming experience is required. Bring a laptop to follow the demonstration and try the R code.

Workshop Materials

Start the hands-on R lab · Prepare R before the session · Download the data · Slides

The lab uses two simulated district-year panels, with seven years and no adjustment covariates. The first has a common treatment start; the second has adoption in Years 5, 6, and 7. All lab graphs and models use raw score points.

Difference-in-Differences for Education Policy

A 45-minute workshop introducing traditional DID, TWFE, event studies, and a brief preview of staggered treatment timing using simulated education-policy data.

Talk Outline

0-10 min
Policy question and data structure. Introduce the education-policy example, the simulated district-year panel, treatment timing, outcome, and comparison group.
10-25 min
Traditional DID. Walk through parallel trends, the 2x2 DID idea, and the simple regression version.
25-38 min
TWFE and event study. Show district and year fixed effects, event-time indicators, and why a single average can hide dynamic effects.
38-45 min
Staggered preview. Preview year-5 and year-8 treatment groups as the transition to Wei's staggered DID section.

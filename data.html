Data Downloads

Updated October 14, 2026. Both datasets have 160 districts observed for seven years (1,120 rows each). They are synthetic education-policy examples with no adjustment covariates. They replace the earlier 11-year workshop datasets.

Both outcome columns represent the same outcome: outcome_raw = 50 + 10 * outcome_std. The lab uses raw score points for graphs and models. The standardized column uses a common pre-treatment reference scale, not separate standardization by group or year. These continuous synthetic scores have no fixed minimum or maximum.

Dataset Purpose Downloads
simple_did_district 80 districts adopt in Year 5; 80 never treated. Constant treatment effect. CSV, Stata
staggered_did_district 40 districts each adopt in Years 5, 6, or 7, and 40 never treated. Effects vary across cohorts and time since adoption. CSV, Stata
data_dictionary Variable names and descriptions. CSV
workshop_estimates Model estimates and standard errors from the current R lab. CSV
csa_weighted_cells Six post-treatment estimates, weights, and contributions to Simple ATT. CSV
data_generation_summary District counts, years, and post-treatment periods by cohort. CSV

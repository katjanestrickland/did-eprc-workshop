Data

These datasets contain 832 simulated districts representing Texas, each observed over seven years

  • Each row represents one district in one year
  • Both datasets are balanced, with no missing district-year observations
  • Treatment is assigned at the district level and continues after adoption
  • Outcomes are measured in standard-deviation units

Codebook

Both downloaded datasets contain only these four variables

Variable Definition
district_id
  • District identifier from 1 to 832
  • The same identifier follows a district across all seven years
year
  • Study year from 1 to 7
  • Not a calendar year
treatment_start_year
  • First treated year is 5 or 6
  • The traditional DID dataset has only Year 5 adopters
  • 0 means never treated
outcome_std
  • Simulated achievement measured in SD units
  • Untreated outcomes are standardized across all districts and years before effects are added
  • The final observed outcome is not standardized again

An effect of 0.20 SD represents an increase equal to one fifth of the untreated outcome’s standard deviation

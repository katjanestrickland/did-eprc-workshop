These datasets contain 832 simulated districts representing Texas, each observed over seven years. The simulation uses parameters estimated from Texas achievement data. For details, see [simulation paper link forthcoming].

Codebook

Each row represents one district in one year

Variable Definition
state
  • TX identifies the state represented by the simulation
district_id
  • Synthetic district identifier from 1 to 832
  • The same identifier follows a district across all seven years
year
  • Study year from 1 to 7
  • Not a calendar year
cohort
  • treated_year5, treated_year6, or never
  • The traditional DID dataset has no Year 6 cohort
treatment_start_year
  • First treated year is 5 or 6
  • 0 means never treated
treated_ever
  • 1 for a district that receives treatment during the study
  • 0 for a never-treated district
treated
  • 1 from the adoption year onward
  • 0 before adoption and for never-treated districts
event_time
  • Study year minus adoption year
  • -1 is the year before adoption and 0 is the first treated year
  • Missing for never-treated districts
outcome_std
  • Simulated achievement measured in SD units
  • The full untreated Texas panel defines the reference mean and SD
  • Treatment effects are added after standardization
  • The observed outcome is not standardized again after treatment

An effect of 0.20 SD means an increase equal to one fifth of the reference standard deviation

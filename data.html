Data

These simulated datasets represent 832 districts observed over seven years, using parameters estimated from Texas SEDA achievement data. They are teaching examples, not actual Texas district records. Neither dataset includes covariates.

Codebook

Both datasets use the same ten variables. Each contains 5,824 district-year observations. District and year jointly identify a row.

Variable Definition
state State whose SEDA-derived parameters calibrate the simulation: TX.
district_id Synthetic district identifier, 1-832. Does not identify an actual district.
year Study year, 1-7; not a calendar year.
cohort Adoption group: treated_year5, treated_year6, or never. The traditional dataset has no Year 6 cohort.
treatment_start_year First treated year: 5 or 6. Zero means never treated.
treated_ever 1 if the district receives treatment during the study; otherwise 0.
treated 1 from adoption onward; 0 before adoption and for never-treated districts.
event_time Year minus adoption year: -1 is the year before adoption, 0 is the first treated year. Missing for never-treated districts.
outcome_std Continuous simulated achievement relative to the mean and SD of the full untreated Texas panel. Effects are added after standardization.
outcome_raw Same outcome in illustrative score points: 50+10×outcome_std50+10\times outcome\_std. Not a real test scale; no fixed minimum or maximum.

Outcome scales: An effect of 0.20 on outcome_std equals 2 points on outcome_raw. The Hands-on R Lab uses outcome_raw. Observed outcomes are not restandardized after treatment.

Only event_time has missing values, for never-treated districts (NA in CSV and system missing in Stata). There are no adjustment covariates. True effects and untreated counterfactuals are retained separately for instructor validation.

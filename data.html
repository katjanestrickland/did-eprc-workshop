Data

These datasets illustrate a district-level intervention in Texas using 832 simulated districts followed over 7 years. Every district is observed each year, so the panels are balanced. Each row represents one district in one year. Once a district starts treatment, it stays treated for the remaining years. Both datasets are ready for analysis and include the treatment indicators used in the lab.

Codebook

Variable Definition
district_id District identifier from 1 to 832
year Study year from 1 to 7
treatment_start_year First treated year, with 0 for never treated
outcome_std Achievement measured in standard-deviation (SD) units
treated_ever 1 in every year for a district that eventually adopts, otherwise 0
treated 1 in the adoption year and all later years, otherwise 0

Both treatment indicators are supplied in the downloads.
